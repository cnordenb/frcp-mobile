# Requirements
0. These requirements apply to the Android app, on phones in portrait, on Android 8.0 and later.
1. A draw will pick one civ at random from the civ pool.
2. A civ will not be drawn again in a round before every civ of the civ pool has been drawn in it.
3. A draw made after every civ of the civ pool has been drawn will start a new round.
4. With continuous freshness on, the last N civs drawn will be kept out of the next draw, also across rounds.
5. The N of continuous freshness will be at most half the size of the civ pool.
6. A swipe left on the Draw tab will draw.
7. A swipe right on the Draw tab will undo.
8. A swipe up on the Draw tab will re-roll.
9. A swipe down on the Draw tab will reset.
10. A tap on the civ icon will play the current civ's jingle.
11. A re-roll will replace the last draw with another civ that it could have been.
12. A reset will end the round.
13. Undo will take back the last draw or reset of the current visit.
14. Redo will restore the last draw or reset that was undone.
15. Undo will not take back a re-roll.
16. A change of the civ pool will clear undo and redo.
17. Clearing the drawn log will clear undo and redo.
18. Loading a share link will clear undo and redo.
19. Loading a preset will clear undo and redo.
20. The drawn log will list the draws newest first.
21. The drawn log will separate the rounds.
22. The remaining log will list the civs of the civ pool not yet drawn this round.
23. The Pool tab will let the user choose the edition.
24. The Pool tab will show every civ of the chosen edition.
25. A tap on a civ on the Pool tab will switch that civ on or off.
26. A DLC toggle will switch all civs of its DLC on or off.
27. The Pool tab will show all its civs without scrolling.
28. The app will write share links that frcp.net opens.
29. The app will open share links written by frcp.net.
30. A share link opened while the picker has content will be previewed before it replaces anything.
31. A share link opened while the picker is empty will load at once.
32. The app will write presets that the desktop app loads.
33. The app will load presets written by the desktop app.
34. The edition, the civ pool and the rounds will be kept between visits.
35. The settings will be kept between visits.
36. The user will be able to turn the jingles off.
37. The user will be able to turn the UI sounds off.
38. With legacy jingles on, a civ that has a legacy jingle will play it instead of its jingle.
